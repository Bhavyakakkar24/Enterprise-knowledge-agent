## 1. Introduction & Company Overview

Welcome to Acme Corp ("the Company"). At Acme Corp, our mission is to deliver intelligent enterprise software solutions that empower organizations globally. We are committed to fostering an inclusive, collaborative, and high-performance work environment.

This Employee Handbook and HR Policy document outlines the terms, benefits, standards of conduct, and workplace guidelines that apply to all personnel. All employees are expected to familiarize themselves with and adhere to the policies detailed herein.

---

## 2. Working Hours & Attendance

### 2.1 Standard Work Week
* **Standard Hours:** The standard work week consists of **40 hours**, typically scheduled Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time.
* **Core Hours:** Acme Corp operates on a **Core Working Hours** model between **10:00 AM and 4:00 PM** in each team's primary time zone. All employees must be accessible for team collaboration, synchronous standups, and client engagements during core hours.
* **Flexible Scheduling:** Employees may adjust their start and finish times outside core hours (e.g., 7:30 AM to 4:00 PM or 10:00 AM to 6:30 PM) upon written agreement with their direct line manager.

### 2.2 Time Tracking and Overtime
* Salaried exempt employees are evaluated based on deliverables, milestone completion, and meeting objectives rather than hourly punch cards.
* Non-exempt hourly employees must log all hours using the Acme People Portal. Overtime work (exceeding 40 hours per week) requires advance written approval from the Department Head and is compensated at 1.5x the base hourly rate.

## 3. Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

Acme Corp embraces a modern, flexible hybrid work philosophy that balances individual autonomy with in-person team connection.

### 3.1 Hybrid Schedule
* **Office & Remote Allocation:** Eligible full-time employees are permitted to work remotely up to **3 days per week**, with a minimum of **2 days per week** in their designated regional Acme office.
* **Anchor Days:** Each department head designates specific in-office "Anchor Days" (typically Tuesdays and Thursdays) to facilitate cross-functional workshops, sprint planning, and team alignment.

### 3.2 Fully Remote Arrangements
* Fully remote arrangements (100% remote) require formal approval from both the Department Vice President and the Chief People Officer.
* Employees on fully remote contracts must maintain a dedicated, ergonomic home workspace with a reliable high-speed internet connection (minimum 50 Mbps download / 10 Mbps upload).

### 3.3 Home Office Equipment & Stipend
* **One-time Setup Allowance:** Acme Corp provides a one-time, non-taxable home office reimbursement stipend of **$500 USD** upon onboarding for ergonomic desks, chairs, external monitors, or peripherals. Receipts must be submitted within 60 days of hire via the Acme Expense Portal.
* **Company Hardware:** The IT department provides a standard laptop (MacBook Pro or ThinkPad), noise-cancelling headset, and security hardware tokens.

### 3.4 Work from Anywhere & Location Constraints
* For legal, tax compliance, and payroll withholding reasons, employees must perform all work from their designated country of employment.
* Temporary international remote work ("Workation") is permitted for up to **15 business days per calendar year**, subject to prior manager approval and compliance review.

## 4. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave Entitlements

Acme Corp believes that rest and rejuvenation are essential for sustainable productivity and employee well-being.

### 4.1 Annual Leave (Vacation)
* **Entitlement:** All regular full-time employees are entitled to **25 paid annual leave days** per calendar year (accrued at **2.08 days per completed month** of service).
* **Part-time Employees:** Part-time staff receive annual leave on a pro-rata basis proportional to their contracted hours.
* **Carryover Policy:** Employees are encouraged to utilize their full leave balance each year. A maximum of **5 unused annual leave days** may be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year. Carried-over days must be utilized before March 31 of that new year, or they will lapse.
* **Request Process:** Leave requests of 3 consecutive days or more must be submitted through the Acme People Portal at least **2 weeks in advance**.

### 4.2 Sick & Medical Leave
* **Paid Sick Days:** Employees receive **10 paid sick leave days** per calendar year, available on January 1st of each year (or on a pro-rata basis for mid-year hires).
* **Notification:** Employees must notify their direct manager via Slack or email prior to 8:30 AM on the day of illness.
* **Medical Certification:** A valid medical certificate from a licensed healthcare practitioner is mandatory for any sick leave absence extending beyond **2 consecutive business days**.
* **Unused Sick Leave:** Unused sick days do not roll over to subsequent years and are not eligible for cash payout upon termination.

### 4.3 Parental Leave
* **Primary Caregivers:** 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.
* **Secondary Caregivers:** 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
* Parental leave can be taken at any point within the first 12 months following the child's birth or official placement.

### 4.4 Bereavement Leave
* Employees are granted up to **5 consecutive paid business days** of bereavement leave in the event of the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, parent, sibling, child).
* Up to **3 consecutive paid business days** are provided for extended family members (grandparent, in-law).

## 5. Employee Expenses & Reimbursement

* **Expense Submission:** Business-related travel, client hospitality, and professional development expenses must be submitted with itemized receipts via the Acme Expense Portal within **30 calendar days** of incurring the expense.
* **Daily Meal Allowance (Per Diem):** While traveling for business, a per diem meal allowance of **$75 USD per day** applies. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless part of an authorized client entertainment dinner.
* **Approval Hierarchy:** Expenses under $1,000 require Direct Manager approval. Expenses exceeding $1,000 require Department Head (Director/VP) sign-off.

## 6. Code of Conduct & Information Security

### 6.1 Confidentiality & Intellectual Property
* All work products, code, documentation, designs, and innovations developed during employment at Acme Corp are the exclusive intellectual property of Acme Corporation.
* Employees must sign and uphold the Acme Confidentiality & Proprietary Information Agreement (CPIA).

### 6.2 Data Security & AI Usage
* Employees must not paste confidential customer data, proprietary source code, or internal financial metrics into unapproved public AI tools.
* The internal Acme Knowledge Agent (running on secure, private enterprise infrastructure) is the designated, authorized tool for internal document querying and productivity assistance.
* Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is strictly mandatory across all corporate accounts and SSO portals.

## 7. Performance Management & Probation

* **Probationary Period:** All new hires undergo a standard **90-day probationary period**. During this period, performance is reviewed at 30, 60, and 90-day intervals.
* **Annual Reviews:** Acme Corp conducts bi-annual performance evaluations in June (mid-year calibration) and December (annual review and compensation adjustments).

---

## 8. Separation of Employment & Notice Periods

### 8.1 Notice Period for Resignation
Employees wishing to resign from their position must provide formal written notice to their Line Manager and Human Resources:
* **During Probationary Period:** A minimum notice period of **14 calendar days** is required.
* **Standard Full-Time Staff (Post-Probation):** A minimum notice period of **30 calendar days (1 month)** is required.
* **Senior Management (Director, VP, Executive level):** A minimum notice period of **60 calendar days (2 months)** is required.

### 8.2 Garden Leave & Pay in Lieu
* Acme Corp reserves the right to place an employee on paid garden leave or provide pay in lieu of notice during the designated notice period.

### 8.3 Offboarding & Asset Return
* On or before the final day of employment, the employee must return all Acme-owned equipment, security tokens, company credit cards, and documentation.
* Final pay, including accrued but unpaid annual leave, will be processed in the next scheduled standard payroll cycle.
